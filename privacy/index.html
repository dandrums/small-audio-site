Track Mixer · Mic Enhancer

Privacy policy

Short version: the apps collect nothing about you. Your audio, files and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Track Mixer and Mic Enhancer, made by Small Audio, do not collect, store or share any personal data. In particular, the apps:

  • have no accounts or sign-in,
  • contain no analytics, advertising or tracking,
  • do not connect to the internet or send anything to us or anyone else.

Microphone access

Both apps ask for microphone access because iOS uses that permission for every audio input, including a USB audio interface.

  • Track Mixer uses your audio interface's inputs for its level meters and, if you turn on Live Input, to send them to your mixes. It never uses the built-in microphone.
  • Mic Enhancer processes the input you're using, normally your audio interface, and plays it back through your outputs in real time.

Audio is processed live as it passes through. Neither app records it, saves it or sends it anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Track Mixer keeps a copy of the audio files you import, plus your shows, mixes, cues and settings.
  • Mic Enhancer keeps your presets and settings.

These are stored only on your device, inside the app. Like other app data, they're included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy; we can't see them. Deleting an app deletes its data from the device.

Show Mode and Shortcuts

Show Mode opens Apple's Shortcuts app to run a shortcut you created, such as one that turns on a Focus. The app only asks Shortcuts to run it by name. It can't see what the shortcut does, and nothing is shared with us.

The App Store

When you download an app, Apple handles the purchase and may give developers anonymous, combined statistics such as download numbers. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Changes

If an update ever changes what the apps do with data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the update is released.
